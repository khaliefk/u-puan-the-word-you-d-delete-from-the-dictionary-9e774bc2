The competition asks for the one word you would erase from the dictionary
forever. A judge reads two words at a time and picks the funnier deletion.

## The word

**Monday** — the day the weekend dies.

Everyone hates Monday. The alarm wins, the week starts whether you are
ready or not, and the word itself carries the whole dread of it. Erase it
from the dictionary and the day still shows up on the calendar, but at
least nobody can name it anymore. That is the joke, and it lands the
moment you read the word.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word `Monday` to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing
else. No gloss, no punctuation. The file holds exactly one word, 6
characters, inside the 1-77 character gate.
